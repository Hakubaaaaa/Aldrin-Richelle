Frequently Asked Questions

The dress code for our wedding is semi-formal. We ask that gentlemen wear a polo or collared shirt, and ladies wear a dress or long gown tailored to our palette of Burgundy, Wine Red, Dusty Mauve, or Rose Pink.

Our invitations are addressed to specific guests as noted on your RSVP. If your invite includes a plus one, it will be reflected in the guest count field.

Yes, complimentary parking is available on-site. The ceremony and the reception are located within the same compound and share the same parking area.

Please arrive at least 30 minutes before the ceremony begins at 3:00 PM to allow time for seating.

We ask for an unplugged ceremony so our photographer can capture every moment. Feel free to snap away during the reception!

The most important thing to us is that you're able to celebrate with us on our wedding day. If you wish to give a gift, a monetary gift is the one we suggest.
